Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is takeout ready after calling?

Most lunch and dinner takeout orders are freshly wok-fired and ready for pickup within 12 to 20 minutes! For large party trays, we appreciate 24 hours advance notice.

Do you have vegetarian or gluten-free options?

Yes! Dishes like our Dry-Fried Szechuan String Beans, Mapo Tofu (vegetarian preparation), Vegetable Lo Mein, and Pad Thai (gluten-free rice noodles) can all be customized. Just mention your preferences when calling.

Is parking available at Morrisville Square?

Yes, there is extensive free parking right in front of C&T Wok inside the Morrisville Square complex.
